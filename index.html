Who I Am

About Me

I am a Mathematical Epidemiologist and Data Scientist working at the intersection of statistical modeling, spatial analysis, climate-health research and software development.

My work focuses on turning complex epidemiological and environmental data into evidence for public health decisions. I work with R, Python, SQL, geospatial methods, Bayesian modeling and reproducible analytical workflows.

I hold an MSc in Mathematical Sciences (Epidemiology) from AIMS Rwanda and a B.Sc. (Hons) in Mathematics and Statistics from the University of Rwanda.
